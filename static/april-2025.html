Grey means we did not measure it. Stars here are gross star events accumulated through April 2025, and the archive never records an unstar, so a repo's count reads lower than its page shows today. Velocity is the month's events divided by its thirty days. The percentile ranks each repo against the 87,029 repos the archive saw starred that month. One caveat: the archive keeps monthly totals, so the day-to-day shape inside each sparkline is interpolated. The live board takes one real reading a day, which is what the gold means on the rest of the site.
